Short Description:
Daily duas of the prophets from the Quran with audio, translations & bookmarks.

Full Description:
Dua-ul-Anbiya brings you the beautiful supplications of the prophets as mentioned in the Quran, all in one elegant and easy-to-use app. Whether you're seeking guidance, comfort, or spiritual inspiration, this app helps you connect with the powerful prayers of Allah's messengers.

Features:

Complete Collection of Prophetic Duas
Browse through all the duas of the prophets mentioned in the Quran, organized by prophet. From Adam (AS) to Muhammad (SAW), explore the heartfelt supplications that shaped history.

Arabic Text with Translations
Each dua is presented with its original Arabic text alongside translations in 6 languages: English, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Urdu, and Turkish.

Audio Recitation
Listen to beautiful audio recitations of each dua. Tap the play button to hear the Arabic pronunciation and reflect on the meaning.

Bookmark Your Favorites
Save your most-loved duas to a dedicated Favorites list for quick access. Tap the heart icon to bookmark any dua instantly.

Copy and Share
Easily copy the Arabic text and translation to your clipboard to share with family and friends or use in your own reflections.

Beautiful, Clean Design
Enjoy a modern, distraction-free interface with smooth swipeable cards. Switch between Light and Dark mode for comfortable reading day or night.

Easy Navigation
Browse the complete index of prophets, swipe through duas within each prophet's collection, jump to any dua with quick page navigation, and access your favorites and settings with one tap.

No Internet Required for Content
All duas, translations, and audio are included in the app. No internet connection needed to read or listen to the duas.

Perfect For:
- Daily spiritual reflection
- Learning Quranic supplications
- Understanding the prayers of the prophets
- Sharing meaningful duas with loved ones
- Building a stronger connection with the Quran

May Allah accept your prayers and bless your journey.
